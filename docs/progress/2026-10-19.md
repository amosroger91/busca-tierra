### 1. Deduped owner-financed listings around Fort Smith (no PR — local app)

The app queries Craigslist, Land Century, Landflip, Landhub, LandWatch, LandAndFarm, and Landmodo, plus one-shot probes of the other public listing sites that answer. A full run wrote `output/properties.csv` with 103 properties and `output/run-report.json`. Every row has a financing signal and a pin inside the 40-mile circle. LandWatch and LandAndFarm are the same CoStar catalog, so those copies share a row. Subdivision lots that the catalog pins on one coordinate, including the Chicken Creek Road tracts in Cookson, stay separate when the street addresses differ. ## Memory / knowledge worth keeping

- LandWatch and LandAndFarm owner-financing pages share one `__SERVER_STATE__` shape. Prices on those pages are already dollars. `page-21` and later come back with `pageIndex` 19, a repeat of page 20.
- Landmodo cards join `data-filename` map pins. The catalog is owner-financed even when a card omits the phrase. Newest Arkansas pages are Cherokee Village, well east of Fort Smith.
- CoStar often pins a whole subdivision on one coordinate. Dedupe has to refuse a merge when both street addresses are present and different.
- Craigslist does not prefix-match. Queries have to be full words (`owner financing`, not `owner financ`).
- The live Gitea host is `https://gitea.kscomputing.com`. `gitea.kscomputing.local` is retired.
